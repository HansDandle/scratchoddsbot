# ScratchScout Reddit Bot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 29, 2026

## Overview

The ScratchScout Reddit Bot ("the Bot") is a utility bot that responds to user commands in Reddit communities by providing publicly available lottery scratch-off odds and prize information.

By interacting with the Bot, you acknowledge and agree to this Privacy Policy.

## Information Collected

The Bot may process the following publicly available Reddit data:

- Reddit usernames
- Comment text containing bot commands
- Comment IDs and thread IDs
- Timestamps associated with interactions

The Bot does not collect:

- Passwords
- Email addresses
- Payment information
- Private messages unless explicitly initiated by the user
- Any non-public Reddit account information

## How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to:

- Detect and process bot commands
- Prevent duplicate replies
- Improve bot reliability and moderation compliance
- Maintain temporary logs for debugging and abuse prevention

## Data Storage

Limited interaction data may be temporarily stored in:

- Redis
- Application logs
- Basic operational databases or caches

Stored data is used only for operation of the Bot and is not sold or shared with third parties.

## Third-Party Services

The Bot may use third-party infrastructure providers, including:

- Reddit API services
- Hosting providers
- Cloud infrastructure services

These services may process data as necessary to operate the Bot.

## No Warranty

Lottery odds and prize information are provided for informational purposes only and may not reflect the most current official lottery data. Users should verify all information with the applicable state lottery authority before making purchasing decisions.

## Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed toward individuals under the age of 18.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically without prior notice. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of any revised policy.
